Build habits. Level up.
Three daily goals, a pixel pet that grows, no account needed.

## Description

Drink water, move, and eat well. Each goal you complete earns XP and coins for a pixel pet that hatches, grows through five stages and takes on the look of the habit you keep best.

- Three daily goals: water, movement and eating. Log water in ml or fl oz; mark movement and eating with one tap.
- Movement can complete itself from Apple Health or Health Connect using your steps or workouts.
- Three daily bonuses: a progress photo, a rotating challenge and a goal in your own words.
- Five pets with their own habitats: Tofu the cat, Juno the owl, Pebble the axolotl, Ori the penguin and Fern the forest sprite. Switch any time and keep your progress.
- Gear to unlock with coins. Evolution paths shaped by your habits.
- A home screen widget with your pet and today's count.
- Works offline. No account, no ads, no subscriptions. Everything stays on your phone, with a backup file you control.

Missed a day? Nothing is lost. YOUchi never punishes you; it just waits.
